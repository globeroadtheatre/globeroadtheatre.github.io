THE PROJECT

ONCE UPON
A TIME IN
AMERICA.

An absurdist exploration of gun culture, violence and the national stories that make the unimaginable seem ordinary.

Moving through physical theatre, satire, puppetry and theatrical play, Right to Arm Bears asks how a society rehearses its beliefs — and what happens when its logic is followed too far.

The work is currently being developed. This page introduces its artistic direction rather than announcing a finished production, cast or performance dates.
