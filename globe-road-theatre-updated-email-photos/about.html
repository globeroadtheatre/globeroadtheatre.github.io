OUR PURPOSE

INTERNATIONAL STORIES.
LOCAL ENCOUNTERS.

Globe Road Theatre is a home for new writing, international voices and immersive theatre in London.

We bring cultures into conversation through performance: international artists, familiar gathering places and work that treats audiences as participants in the making of meaning.

Our ambition is simple and expansive. To cultivate creative spaces across language, culture and borders, and build a global community in East London.

THE POP-UP PUB THEATRE OF EAST LONDONOUR ETHOS
✳
“There is no separation between the pub and the theatre, its patrons and its artists.”

We are interested in what happens when art and quotidian life coexist. A bar becomes a stage. Strangers become an ensemble. A local night out becomes an encounter with the wider world.

ARTISTIC LEAD · DIRECTOR · WORKSHOP FACILITATOR

MARK DIAZ

A Los Angeles-born, London-based theatre director working across cultural traditions, physical performance and international collaboration.

Mark's practice is rooted in Total Theatre: embodied performance, ensemble-led stage making and stories where cultures collide. His directing work includes Narukami, Once on Rumspringa and The Cyclops of East London.

He trained at Pomona College, studied directing with Katie Mitchell at Columbia University, and completed an MFA in Theatre Directing at East 15 Acting School.
