Globe Road Theatre is an invitation to exchange ideas, make work and create community across borders.

We welcome conversations with playwrights, performers, designers, movement artists, directors, independent producers, presenting venues and community organisations. Particularly those interested in international voices, intimate spaces, physical performance and work that brings people together.
